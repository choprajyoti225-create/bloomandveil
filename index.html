Questions couples ask

How far in advance should we book?+

Most couples book nine to fourteen months out, especially for Saturdays between May and October. Off-season and weekday dates can sometimes be arranged with a few months' notice.

Do you travel outside the Hudson Valley?+

Yes. A travel fee applies beyond 25 miles of Kingston, calculated at booking so there are no surprises. Overnight stays are billed at cost for weddings past 90 miles.

What if it's hot, humid, or I cry during the vows?+

Everything used is long-wear and waterproof-rated where it matters — base, brows, and mascara. A small touch-up kit is left with your maid of honor for the reception.

Can you match my skin tone if I have a deep complexion?+

Odalys carries a 40-shade professional range and custom-mixes when needed. Send a few unfiltered photos in daylight before the trial so she can prep the right formulas in advance.

Check my date

Send your wedding date, venue, and a little about the day. Odalys replies personally, usually within two business days, with availability and next steps for booking a trial.
